# Scripted push test: push-test

No learning: each robot walks straight at 0.8 m/s, heading held, keeping its lateral slot relative to the object (pair: ANYmal at +slot, Spot at -slot; alone: centred, the arch: one pillar). Broadside push, 20 s, 2 placements per case, nominal object sizes. Distance counts until the first fall of a pushing robot. Training needs about 0.1 m/s on average (3-6 m in at most 80 s, with approach and turning).
